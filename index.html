COSMIC EXPERIENCE

COSMIC is a deep-space astronomical observation console bringing NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) to life through cinematic science and atmospheric design.

Every single day, NASA publishes a new image or video of our fascinating universe alongside explanations written by professional astronomers.

Built with Vanilla Web Technologies, Vite, HTML5 Canvas, and NASA Open APIs for the Hack Club Stardance Mission.
